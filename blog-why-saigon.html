Why our first collection starts in Saigon

Every brand has to start somewhere. Ours started on a plastic stool, with an iced coffee and a notebook full of plans.

Paper Land is designed in Adelaide, but the idea began in Saigon — a city that wakes up early, loudly, and with a coffee in hand. Mornings there run on small rituals: the same café, the same stool, the same order, and a few minutes to write down what the day needs to become.

We wanted our first notebooks to hold on to that feeling. So instead of a logo on the front, each cover carries a single word and a short definition — the way you'd actually explain that word to a friend.

Three words, one morning

Iced coffee is the start. Alleyway is the detour where the good ideas hide. Street food is the reward — a little bit of everything, with crunch. Together they make the Saigon Mornings collection.

Why bring it to Australia?

Because the ritual travels. An Adelaide morning looks different — a flat white instead of an iced coffee, a tram instead of a motorbike — but the habit of sitting down and writing out the day is the same. Our second collection, Adelaide Arvo, is the other half of that story.
