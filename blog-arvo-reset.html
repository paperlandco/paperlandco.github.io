The five-minute arvo reset

arvo

A one-page spread for the 3pm slump: what is done, what is next, and what can honestly wait.

Around mid-afternoon, most to-do lists stop being useful. Some tasks are finished, some have changed, and a few were never going to happen today. The arvo reset is a quick way to take stock without starting a whole new list.

The spread

  1. Done. Write down three things you finished today, however small.
  2. Next. Pick the one task that would make tomorrow morning easier, and do only that.
  3. Later. Move everything else to a "maybe-tomorrow" list — without guilt.
  4. Cuppa. Put the kettle on. This step is not optional.

Dot grid pages work best because you can draw three quick boxes and fill them in. Five minutes, one page, and the rest of the afternoon feels lighter.
