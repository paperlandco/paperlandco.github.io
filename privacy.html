Privacy Policy

Last updated 19 November 2026.

What we collect

Paper Land uses Google Analytics 4 (through Google Tag Manager) to understand how visitors use this website: which pages are viewed, which products are opened or added to the cart, which buttons are clicked and how far pages are scrolled. Google Analytics uses cookies and collects information such as your approximate location, device and browser. It does not give us your name or email address.

Why we collect it

We use this information only to improve the website and our product range, for example to see which collections people browse most.

Your choices

You can block analytics cookies in your browser settings or install the Google Analytics opt-out add-on. This website is a university study project and the contact and newsletter forms do not store the details you type.
