Notebook and paper gifts for people who love to write

A paper notebook is one of the easiest gifts to get right. Here is how to choose one by the person you are buying for.

A good notebook is personal without being risky. It needs no size, it does not go off, and most people who write by hand will use one. The trick is matching the notebook and paper to the person.

For the student

A lined A5 notebook for lecture notes, and something small for to-do lists. Street Food and Brekkie are lined. Pocket Notes is a small spiral notebook that sits beside a laptop.

For the planner

An undated planner is safer than a dated one: they can start it the day they open it, not only in January. Someday covers 12 months with no dates printed.

For the coffee lover

The Saigon Mornings Set brings iced coffee, alleyway and street food together in one gift box. We also stock the Crabit Coffee Series, coffee-themed notebooks from the Vietnamese stationery brand Crabit, in the shop.

For someone who misses Australia

The Adelaide Arvo Set puts three Aussie words on three covers: arvo, brekkie and cuppa. It travels well in a suitcase.

For the sketcher

Plain pages and heavier paper. Cuppa has no lines and 120gsm ivory paper, so pencils and fineliners behave.

Make it personal

  • Write a short note on the first page. It turns a notebook into a keepsake.
  • Choose the word on the cover for the person: brekkie for the early riser, someday for the dreamer.
  • Add a pen they will actually use. Our notebook paper guide explains which pens suit which paper.

Shipping

We ship Australia-wide from Adelaide, free on orders over $60. See the help page for current dispatch times before you count on a delivery date.
