Collections

Three chapters,
one ritual.

Each collection is a chapter in the same story — the small daily rituals that start with a word and a blank page. Start in Saigon, slow down in Adelaide, and plan for someday.

Every Paper Land notebook is an A5 hardcover with 160 numbered pages of 120gsm ivory paper, in dot grid, lined or plain, with a single word and its definition on the cover. Alongside our own collections we stock the Coffee Series from Crabit, a stationery brand from Vietnam. Prefer to give them together? Each of our first two collections also comes as a three-notebook gift set in a recycled kraft box.
